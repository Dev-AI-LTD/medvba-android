## Frequently asked questions

### How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap **Forgot password** and follow the instructions sent to your email.

### Why can’t I start a quiz?

Free accounts have a daily question limit. Upgrade to **Premium** in the app, or try again the next day.

### How do I restore purchases?

Open the **Premium** / paywall screen and tap **Restore Purchases**. Use the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple:

**Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → MEDVBA**

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app: **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. Deletion is permanent and removes your profile, quiz history, and associated data.

### Study mode and audio

Open **Study**, choose a module and chapter, then read the summary or tap **Play** for recorded audio (where available). Some chapters also offer text-to-speech on your device if audio is unavailable.

### AI Tutor

Open **AI Tutor** from the tab bar to ask questions about anatomy, physiology, pathology, and pharmacology. AI answers are for study support only and do not replace clinical training or official teaching.

### Chat with other students

Use the **Chats** tab to message classmates and find study partners. MEDVBA does not require live video sessions for study.

### The app crashes or won’t load

1. Update MEDVBA to the latest version from the App Store.  
2. Restart your iPhone or iPad.  
3. Check your internet connection.  
4. If the problem continues, email **contact@devaieood.com** with your device model and iOS version.
