### Why MEDVBA?

Medical students need more than static PDFs. MEDVBA combines **practice**, **understanding**, and **motivation** in one app:

- **Quiz** — train with multiple-choice questions across major anatomy modules  
- **Study** — read chapter summaries and listen to recorded audio before you test yourself  
- **AI Tutor** — ask questions about anatomy, physiology, pathology, and pharmacology  
- **Progress** — streaks, statistics, achievements, and leaderboards  
- **Chat** — message classmates and find study partners in the app  

---

### Features

**30,000+ quiz questions**  
Quick quizzes, practice sessions, and exam-style simulations. Modules include upper and lower limbs, internal organs, head and neck, neuroanatomy, medical school entrance exam content, and more. Every question includes explanations.

**Chapter Study with audio**  
Structured summaries per chapter. Listen to recorded voice on many chapters, then start a chapter quiz from the same screen.

**AI Tutor**  
Get instant, clear explanations when you are stuck. Available from the dedicated tab in the app.

**Track your progress**  
See accuracy, questions answered, and study time. Stay motivated with streaks and achievements.

**Premium (optional)**  
Unlock unlimited daily questions, full Study content, advanced statistics, AI tutor, and more. Subscriptions are managed through the App Store or Google Play.

**Languages**  
English, Romanian, Portuguese, and Spanish.

### Who is it for?

- Medical school applicants preparing for entrance exams  
- Medical and nursing students learning anatomy  
- Anyone revising gross anatomy, neuroanatomy, and related sciences
